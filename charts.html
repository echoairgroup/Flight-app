What you can use it for

The official MSFS 2024 planner provides the aviation planning environment used alongside Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024.

Plan and discover routes
View aviation charts and maps
Check METAR and TAF
Find radio navigation information

Account & access

Microsoft handles authentication directly. Flight App does not ask for or store your Microsoft, Xbox or PlayStation credentials.

Secure Microsoft authentication

When you launch the official planner, sign in using the account linked to your Microsoft Flight Simulator 2024 installation.

Flight App Integration

Everything around the official planner.

Airport Search Search airports by ICAO code, airport name, city or country.
Route Preparation Select your departure, arrival and optional alternate before launching the official planner.
Great-circle Calculation Flight App calculates approximate distance and initial bearing between your selected airports.
Local Route Memory Your prepared route can be saved locally so it remains available when you return to the page.
